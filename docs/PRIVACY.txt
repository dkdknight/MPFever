MPFever - privacy policy
========================

MPFever has no account, no advertising and no statistics: it collects nothing about you for itself. This page lists every
connection MPFever makes, what it sends, and how to turn off those it makes by itself. MPFever shows a short version of it
the first time it starts.


1. Connections MPFever makes by itself
--------------------------------------

Most of them can be turned off in MPFever's ⚙ Settings window. They are kept in mpfever_settings.txt, next to MPFever.exe
(one "name=value" per line), where the settings named here can also be written by hand.

- Updates: at start, MPFever asks GitHub (api.github.com, github.com) for its newest version, and downloads it when there
  is one. GitHub sees your IP address, as for any visit. Turn off: autoupdate=0 (autoupdate=ask offers updates first).

- Your public address (hosts only): to show the address friends type to join, the host's MPFever asks api.ipify.org for
  its public IP address. ipify sees your IP address. Turn off: lookupip=0 (the local address is shown instead).

- Opening the port on your router (hosts only): when you host, MPFever asks your router (UPnP or NAT-PMP, on your own
  network only) to forward the game's port to your PC, and removes the forwarding when the session ends or MPFever closes.
  Turn off: upnp=0 (then forward the port by hand, or join through Steam).

- The list of public servers (mpfever.knight-hd.fr, run by MPFever's author, behind Cloudflare):
  - the multiplayer page reads the list while its « Public servers » tab is open; the list service sees your IP address
    and keeps no record of who reads the list;
  - a dedicated server announces itself there every 30 seconds while its world runs: its name, message of the day, world,
    company mode, MPFever version and game build, number of players and maximum, whether it asks for a password, its port,
    the address given by public-address= (else the IP address it connects from) and its Steam ID (so that players can join
    it through Steam). The service checks that the server can be reached, shows these to every player, forgets the server
    90 seconds after its last announce, and writes in its log the server's name, address and port when it appears,
    disappears or becomes reachable or not (the log keeps the last 2 to 4 MB). Turn off: public=false in
    mpfever_server.properties keeps a server out of the list; serverlist=0 in mpfever_settings.txt turns the list off
    entirely.


2. Connections you ask for
--------------------------

- Playing together: hosting, joining a game, a dedicated server. The players' games exchange what the game needs (builds,
  game time, chat, player and company names, mod list, savegames) with the host or the server you chose, directly or
  through Steam (Steam networking: Valve sees the connection, as for any Steam game).

- Missing mods: when you join a game, the game's Mod Hub installs the host's mods you lack (turn off: automods=0), and
  the host can send you the mods the Mod Hub does not have.

- « Report a problem »: MPFever puts the logs of the session in a zip in its reports folder. Before writing the zip, it
  takes out of the text logs your Windows account's and PC's names, your Steam account numbers, public IP addresses and
  e-mail addresses. Player names, savegame names and the game's settings stay: the logs are about them.
  Since 0.7.13, the game's crash dump (a copy of the game's memory, which may hold addresses and account numbers that
  cannot be taken out) and the session's chat are never put in a report, nor kept from another player's report.
  Since 0.7.9, in a session, the zip also holds the other players' reports (made the same way on their PCs and sent through
  the session's connections), and it is sent to MPFever's report service (mpfever.knight-hd.fr, the server list's PC), which
  keeps it 60 days and opens a public GitHub issue with a link to it, in the project's name. GitHub issues and their links are
  public. Turn off: reportshare=0 in mpfever_settings.txt (your report is never sent for another player's, and you ask for
  none), reportupload=0 (nothing is sent: the zip is shown and a new GitHub issue opens in your browser, to post yourself,
  as before 0.7.9).

- Links (Discord, Ko-fi, Nexus Mods, GitHub): they open in your browser only when you click them.


3. On your PC
-------------

MPFever writes its settings, logs, reports and session files next to MPFever.exe and in the game's folders, and installs
its files in the game's folder (see README.txt, "UNINSTALL", to remove them). They stay on your PC.


4. Questions
------------

Open an issue on https://github.com/dkdknight/MPFever/issues. The source code of every part of MPFever, including the list
service (serverlist folder), is published there under the GNU General Public License.
