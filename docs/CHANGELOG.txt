MPFever - Changelog
===================

0.3.3-experimental - companies kept, contracts and shared stations, fewer desyncs
  - FIX (companies): after a resynchronisation (a player joining, a difference), the request of a player
    to find their company again could arrive while the host's game was still loading and was lost: the
    host's game then had no company for that player, everything the player built was built for the
    host's company and saved so (the player came back with a new, empty company). The messages that
    arrive during the load are now read. The state check no longer takes a difference of companies,
    money, owners, loans, roads, constructions or lines for a "local measure" after a reload.
  - NEW: a company belongs to the player's Steam account (and name): a player finds their company
    again even after changing their name. Two players announcing themselves at the same moment get
    their companies in the same order on every game.
  - FIX (desync): a depot or station joined to an existing street junction (without cutting the street)
    was rebuilt on the other games with its own separate entrance node (not connected to the street):
    the construction is now attached to the right node (the engine's numbering of the construction's
    nodes was the cause). Reproduced and checked on the savegame of the report.
  - FIX (desync): the builds of another player could land one simulation step late on a game running
    fast (x4): every game now stops a little before such a build and steps exactly to its time.
  - FASTER: the state check every 10 seconds took up to 0.9 s of simulation (a hiccup every 10 s);
    it now takes about 0.05 s.
  - NEW: the window of a station, depot, vehicle, line or building shows its owner company.
  - NEW: contracts per company. The offers stay common; an accepted contract belongs to the company
    that accepted it: only its lines count for it, its money and its better income go to that company;
    each game shows only its own company's contracts (and the offers it did not decline).
  - NEW: shared stations with a fee. The owner of a station sets in the station window a fee per stop
    (- / +, 0 = not shared). The other companies' lines can only stop at shared stations; every stop
    of their vehicles there pays the fee to the owner (both games book it at the same moment).
  - NEW: trees and plants of the brush are rebuilt on the other games (same models at the same places,
    with a construction of the mod; they are left out of the state check). First version, not yet tried
    with the real brush: native.log/mod.log say what happened ("assets placed", "ASSET REPLAY").
  - Ground paint: still not copied (diagnostics in native.log: "terrain: command without height cells").
  - Autotest scenarios: contract, share, fees, wait, assettest, apiprobe, treeprobe.

0.3.2-experimental - companies mode: loans, depot desync
  - FIX (desync): a depot or station built against a street without cutting it (the new street end joins an existing
    junction) could not be rebuilt on the other games ("Unknown exception" at the command): the games drifted apart
    and the host reloaded its game. It is now rebuilt in two steps (street part and construction together, then
    separately) when the direct way fails. Reproduced and checked on a real savegame.
  - NEW: every company has its own loans. Before, the game's loan window only worked for the host's company (the
    others' requests were ignored). Each other company now has its own offers, loans taken and monthly payments, kept
    in the companies list of the savegame, with the game's own loan rules. The loan window of a player shows
    their company's loans. The state check also compares the loans of every company.
  - Contracts (subsidies): the game keeps ONE list of contracts for the host's company; proposals, acceptance and
    completion of other companies' contracts are not available yet (the clicks of a company other than the
    host's are ignored instead of accepting the host's contract for everybody). Own contracts per company need
    the notifications of the game to be split per company too: planned.
  - Autotest scenario "loan".

0.3.1-experimental - companies mode: tools, unique buildings, money popups
  - FIX (one company per player): what a player builds with the construction tools (buildings, depots, stations,
    roads, tracks...) is now built, paid and owned by THEIR company. Before, the tools of a player other than the
    host built for the host's company (the cost was taken from the host's money and the building stayed the host's,
    the player's own money stayed untouched). How: the interface's tools always issue their builds for the
    savegame's company; the native module now replaces that company by the player's own in the build it holds
    before it is applied (native.log: "tool build from ...").
  - FIX: the permits of the game's unique buildings (headquarters, landmarks...) are counted per company: a player
    can build their own headquarters even if another player already has theirs ("Already built" only means that
    YOUR company has built it). Done in the game's company modules, in every Lua state that reads them.
  - FIX: the money shown above the buildings (earned / lost) no longer piles up on the games that do not play that
    company: the engine only empties the figures of the local company, the native module now empties the other
    companies' too, before the interface can show them.
  - FIX: headquarters flag of a construction is copied to the other games.
  - A depot or station that could not be rebuilt on the other games ("Unknown exception" at the command) is tried
    again without the node configurations and id maps of the tool's proposal (the engine recomputes them).
  - mod.log / native.log now tell: the permits counted for the company ("permits count"), who a captured build is
    for ("capture: owner references"), what the native module changed in a tool's build.

0.3.0-experimental - one company per player
  - NEW: the host can choose, when hosting (MPFever window of the main menu), between "One company for
    everybody" (as before) and "One company per player". In the second mode every player plays their own company
    on the same map: own money, own roads, stations, depots, vehicles and lines. The host keeps the company of
    the savegame, a new player gets a new company with a starting capital (2,000,000; companystart=N in
    mpfever_settings.txt changes it). The company belongs to the player's name: after a reconnection or in a later session on the same
    savegame the same name finds the same company (the list of companies is kept in the savegame).
  - Colours: each company has its own colour (given to its player entity); the lines it creates take it
    (a little darker for each line it already has), so lines can be told apart on the map and in the lists.
  - How: the game's own scripts (loans, company level, ticket prices...) always work for ONE company, so every
    game's simulation sees the savegame's company as "the player", while the interface and the construction tools
    of each game act as that game's own company (the native module writes it into the game state). Every action
    travels with its company: what the other games rebuild is built and paid for by that company, as the same
    entities in the same places (checked by the state check, which now also compares who owns what and the money
    of every company).
  - Tested on two games: roads, upgrades, road types, stops, vehicle purchases, lines, commands, terrain edits,
    each by the host and by the client, and a resynchronisation in the middle (the company is found again):
    same owners and same money of every company on both games, no desync.
  - Limits of the first version (also in README): loans and the Company window's actions only work for the host's
    company (the others' are ignored), the level of the savegame's company is shared, no money transfer between
    companies.
  - A stroke of the ground painter (it contains nothing a script can see) is no longer shipped to the other games
    as an empty build, which could be refused there and make the host reload its game. The paint itself is still
    not copied. The tree/asset brush is not copied either; the mod now logs what these brush constructions contain
    (mod.log: "asset construction") to prepare it.
  - native.log also lists, once per kind, the grids found in commands without height cells (ground paint...).
  - FIX: bulldozing a street that carries stops (bus or tram stops) was not copied to the other games ("stop not
    replicable": the street stayed there and the games drifted apart until a resynchronisation). It is now
    replayed with the engine's own removal proposal, which removes the stops with the street.

0.2.10-experimental - terrain edits are copied, no more reload
  - Raising / lowering / levelling the ground is now copied to the other players like any other
    construction, by both the host and the clients. Before, a terrain edit made the host reload
    its game for everybody (a resynchronisation that takes time) and a client's edit stayed local.
    How: a script cannot read or write the cells of a terrain edit, so the native module copies
    them when the engine applies the edit (the cells are packed; at worst about 11 bytes of text per cell),
    the mod ships them with the game time, and on the other games the module copies them into an
    empty ground edit of the same size as it enters the engine's command list. The edit is made at
    once on the game of the player who edits (the terrain tool cannot be held like the others)
    and at the same game time on the others when they have not passed it yet, otherwise a step
    later (logged as LATE). The cells are first handed to the native module, which confirms, and only
    then is the empty ground edit sent, so a failure can never flatten the ground. Tested on two games: a 12x12 and a 300x300 cells patch with uneven
    heights, both directions, speed 1 and 4, paused game: same ground afterwards (sum of the
    heights of the patch identical), no desync.
  - New check in the state comparison: "terrain" (the sum of the checksums of the edits applied).
    A lost or doubled edit shows in the next check and reloads the host's game.
  - If the native module cannot copy an edit (unknown game build, unreadable cells) the old
    behaviour remains: the host's game is reloaded for everybody. A terrain edit that comes with
    other construction work, ground paint and the trees brush are still not copied (logged); neither is an
    edit of more than about 3 million cells (the host's game is reloaded then).
  - The command audit now also separates the commands of the simulation loop from the others on
    build 40420 (the address was only known for 40408).
  - Diagnostics for the next tests: native.log now says, once per tool, with which options the game's
    construction tools build ("build context from ...": terrain alignment, street graph cleanup,
    gathering of buildings...). It is meant to find out why a road built from an existing junction can leave
    a slightly different lane-connection setup on the other games ("nodeConfigs" in the state check).

0.2.9-experimental - game update 40420
  - Transport Fever 3 was updated (build 40408 -> 40420): the native module did not recognise the
    new game and stayed off, which made the multiplayer button disappear from the main menu and
    switched off the other native features. The module now knows both builds. The multiplayer page
    and the script-thread patch are also looked up by their code, so they survive small game
    updates; the construction hooks still need the build to be listed (any other build: hooks off,
    the mod then runs without them and reports it in native.log).

0.2.8-experimental - terrain tool crash fixed, waiting marker, command audit, small fixes
  - While a construction waits for its game time (about two seconds), a translucent
    yellow circle shows on the ground where you clicked, then disappears when the
    construction is built (it was not obvious that the order had been taken).
  - Command audit: the native module counts, for each kind of command, how many the
    simulation applied, and the launcher copies the counts to logs\audit-NAME.txt every
    30 seconds. Comparing two players' files shows an action that one game applied and
    the other never did, i.e. something the mod does not synchronise (tool: re\audit_compare.py).
  - README: the sentence saying MPFever connects nowhere else was inexact: when hosting it
    asks api.ipify.org for your public IP address (for Steam invitations). It can now be
    switched off with lookupip=0 in mpfever_settings.txt (invitations then carry the local
    address). Added: a savegame made without the mod works (same mods needed by everyone).
  - Fixed a crash of the host's game when using the terrain tool (the tool's command was
    held like the others; that tool does not survive it). Terrain edits are not held any more.
    They cannot be copied to the other games: a host's terrain edit makes the other games
    reload the host's game (automatic resynchronisation); a client's edit stays local.
  - Research for the next versions (no change in play): extra player companies are possible in the
    engine (see re\FINDINGS.txt).

0.2.6-experimental - wider synchronisation check
  - The state check every 10 seconds now also compares what the players set on lines
    (name, stops, loading mode, waiting times), the names of stations, lines and
    vehicles, which line each vehicle is on and whether the player stopped it or is
    selling it, the lane connections / crosswalks / traffic lights of the road nodes,
    and the parameters of every construction (the modules of a modular station).
    A difference in any of them used to go unnoticed.
  - Tested on two games, identical afterwards: renaming a line and a vehicle, a line
    colour, stopping and restarting a vehicle, reversing one, sending one to a depot,
    changing a line's waiting times (and, tested before: buying vehicles, creating a
    line).
  - A difference in the road nodes' configuration (lane connections, crosswalks,
    traffic lights) is written to the log and only reloads the game when it lasts
    (5 minutes); the other new checks reload the game like the existing ones.

0.2.5-experimental - noise barriers on tracks
  - A noise barrier on a track built by another player no longer appears in the middle
    of the track. The tracks rebuilt on the other games had lost their distance from the
    axis (5 m for the tracks the tool lays), which is where the barrier is placed. The
    distance is now copied, and the synchronisation check compares it.

0.2.4-experimental - tram on roads, walls, menu
  - Tram tracks laid on an existing road are now reproduced on the other players'
    games (the road kept its template, only its lanes change; the other games used to
    rebuild the lanes from the template and lose the tram).
  - A noise barrier placed on a track is put on the right side of the track on every
    game, whichever way each game's segment runs.
  - The checkpoint compares the decorations (side, model) and the vehicles each lane
    takes, independently of the segment's direction.
  - The MPFever window now closes as soon as the game starts to load (host: when
    hosting starts; player: when the host's game is received). It stayed over the
    game before, as a leftover that could not be closed.

0.2.3-experimental - road decorations, signals, noise barriers
  - Rail signals placed on an existing track are now reproduced on the other players'
    games (they were refused with an engine error).
  - Noise barriers added to an existing track or road are now reproduced (the other
    games only received the same track again, without the barrier).
  - The MPFever window closes by itself once the game is loaded and connected (it used
    to stay over the game, with a close button that did nothing).
  - A road built with trees (or any other road decoration) now keeps them on the other
    players' games. The decorations were not sent, so the road appeared bare.
  - The synchronisation check now compares road decorations too, so that a difference
    is detected instead of going unnoticed.
  - A build that the engine refuses (collision...) no longer makes the other players
    wait half a minute before their next build: the player who built tells the others
    at once that nothing is coming.
  - The checkpoint compares more of every road (style, type, decorations).
  - The dust cloud of a construction going up is now shown on the other players' games
    too (it used to appear only for the player who placed the building).
  - Road upgrades that move town buildings: the buildings are now placed exactly where
    the player's game put them (the other games used to pick other places).
  - Known issue: when a road upgrade moves several town buildings, the town may still
    place one extra building differently on each game. The host's automatic
    resynchronisation then corrects it after a few seconds.

0.2.1-experimental - constructions on streets, reconnection
  - Depots and stations built against a street (road and tram depots, street stations)
    are now reproduced exactly on the other players' games, entrance included. They
    used to be refused by the engine and only corrected by a resynchronisation.
  - A build that cannot be reproduced makes the host reload its game for everybody at
    once (no more waiting for two failed checks and a two-minute delay).
  - Lost connection: the player's MPFever reconnects to the host by itself (for 5
    minutes) and receives the host's game again.
  - Any savegame can be hosted, even one created without MPFever: the mod is added to
    it automatically when it is loaded for a multiplayer game.
  - Menu: larger MPFever window, visible outlined "Multiplayer" button on the left.
  - Builds that could not be reproduced now log what they contain (models, heights,
    segment types), to make bug reports useful.

0.2.0-experimental - multiplayer from the game's main menu
  - MPFever.exe starts the game directly. A "Multiplayer (MPFever)" button in the
    game's main menu opens the MPFever window: player name, host one of your
    savegames, or join a host by its address. No more launcher buttons to click.
  - The host's savegame is sent automatically to every player who joins and loads by
    itself (no file to copy, no "Start" button: the session starts when the host's
    game is loaded). A player joining later is welcomed the same way.
  - Steam invitations: the host can invite friends from the MPFever window, and
    friends can use "Join game" in their Steam friends list. A friend whose game is
    closed: Steam starts it and MPFever joins the host by itself.
  - The old launcher window stays available with MPFever.exe --dev.

0.1.1-experimental - synchronisation fixes
  - Random numbers of the game mechanics (loan offers, contracts, events) are now
    identical in every game: no more divergence of loans after a few minutes.
  - False alarms removed from the state comparison (internal engine counters), which
    caused needless resynchronisations at the start of a session.
  - No more speed request loop after a resynchronisation.
  - Notification sounds are no longer played twice.
  - Constructions from a previous session are no longer sent again at startup.
  - Lines: stops can be added in the line editor again (results of an earlier
    session no longer hide the new ones); lines and every other action now work
    while the game is paused, on every player's side.
  - Removing a stop with the bulldozer is replicated (the stop used to stay on the
    other players' maps).
  - Fewer interruptions: a slight drift of the engine's own simulation (vehicle
    positions, passengers, town statistics) is reloaded only if it lasts 5 minutes;
    real differences (roads, stops, lines, buildings, finances) still trigger an
    immediate resynchronisation.
  - Root cause of the slow drift found and fixed: on processors with 8 threads or
    more, the engine updates the game scripts (towns, loans, industries...) in
    parallel, in an order that depends on thread timing. The native module now gives
    them their own pool with one worker, as the engine itself does on smaller
    processors. Only the scripts' update is affected: the simulation, the rendering
    and the loading keep every core. Result in testing: 7 minutes at x4 without a
    single difference, and the same speed as before.
  - Actions applied a few steps late on another player's game at x4 (a game could
    pass its stop point while the interface reacted): actions are now stamped with
    a reserve for it, and games stop exactly at their stop points (exact steps).
    This was the cause of vehicles drifting after purchases and line changes.
  - Fewer synchronised slowdowns: wider waiting window between games.
  - Road upgrades that move a town building: the building stays a town building on
    the other players' games (it used to become the player's construction).
  - The game's speed request at startup no longer floods the launcher log.

0.1.0-experimental - first public test version
  - Cooperative multiplayer in one shared company, connection by IP address.
  - Synchronisation of the game's construction tools (roads, tracks, joins in the
    middle of a road, upgrades, stops including on tram tracks, stations, depots,
    buildings, bulldozer), vehicle purchases, lines, assignments, speed and pause.
  - Host authority: state comparison every 10 seconds (money, loans, company, contracts,
    towns, industries, cargo, vehicles, roads), automatic money correction, automatic
    resynchronisation from the host's savegame (about 20 seconds).
  - Deterministic game mechanics: contracts, loans, weather and industry events are
    the same in every game.
  - Launcher in English and French (follows the Windows language).
  - No process injection: the native module is a winhttp.dll the game loads by itself.
  - Full source code published, with build instructions.
